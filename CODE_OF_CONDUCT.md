# Code of Conduct

KubeRescue should be a respectful and welcoming project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Make space for people with different experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeated disruption of project discussions

## Reporting

Report code of conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer.
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participants when needed
to keep the project healthy.
